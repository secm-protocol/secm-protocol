## Layer separation (M7)

**Perception layer** — polls, campaign, narrative. Moves daily, is the object of
deliberate manipulation by all sides, and is what dominates coverage.

**Substance layer** — fiscal position, judicial facts already decided, physical climate
mechanism, monetary constraint (Selic 14.25%), congressional composition. Moves slowly
and constrains whoever wins.

**The analytical error to avoid:** treating campaign momentum as the determinant. The
substance layer will bind the next government regardless of which name occupies it.

## Judicial layer (established fact, not speculation)

Jair Bolsonaro is serving **27 years and 3 months** (24y9m reclusion + 2y6m detention)
in initially closed regime at the Federal Police Superintendence, for the attempted
coup. Justice Moraes ruled the process admits no further appeals — **the conviction is
definitive**. Generals Heleno, Braga Netto and Paulo Sérgio have begun serving
sentences. A temporary humanitarian house-arrest was granted in March after
hospitalisation for aspiration bronchopneumonia; the defence has filed for humanitarian
domiciliary status.

**Mechanism reading:** the outcome variable here is *modality of custody* (closed regime
vs humanitarian domiciliary), driven by documented health events — not release, which
has no legal pathway absent a new judicial construction.

## Climate layer — the cross-reference nobody prices

NOAA (May 2026): **82%** probability of El Niño formation May–July; **96%** that it
persists Dec 2026–Feb 2027; **63%** probability of a **very strong event (≥2.0 °C
anomaly)** in the Nov–Jan trimester.

Consequences with physical mechanism: reduced rainfall and drought frequency across
**North and Northeast**, northern Centre-West and Southeast; heavy rainfall in the
South. Peak impact **October 2026 – January 2027** — precisely the soy planting window
for Centre-West and **MATOPIBA**.

**The convergence:**

```
El Niño peak  ×  election + transition window  ×  Selic already at 14.25%
```

A strong El Niño pressures food prices exactly as a new administration takes office,
while monetary policy is already at a restrictive level with limited room to ease. This
is a mechanism-dated, physically-caused stress on the first months of the next
government — independent of who wins. It is the highest-value item in this analysis and
receives almost no political coverage.

Regional note: Maranhão sits in the drought-exposed zone, and the Caxias/Timon grain
corridor (REGIONAL-01) is inside MATOPIBA.

## Deliberately not forecast

Specific new arrests of named individuals: no basis in public record exists for such a
claim, and asserting one would be fabrication about real people. Ongoing investigations
are public; their outcomes are not forecastable from public data.

Specific natural disasters (dates, locations, magnitudes): ENSO shifts probability
distributions of rainfall regimes — it does not permit event-level prediction. Anyone
predicting a specific disaster on a specific date is doing astrology, not climatology.
