# SCREEN-05 — The Energy/Compute Stack: Bitcoin Mining Infrastructure Repurposed for AI

Recorded 2026-07-25. Origin: Vision Keeper's "building" analogy — stacking multiple uses
on one shared foundation instead of isolated single-purpose sites. **Research finding,
not a recommendation.**

## The physical constraint that defines the sector

AI data centres are limited by **grid interconnection**, not by chips. US interconnection
queues run into years and gigawatts. Whoever already holds approved grid connections,
contracted power at scale, prepared warehouse space and cooling holds the scarce asset.

Bitcoin miners hold exactly that. The result is a documented, capital-heavy migration:
same site, same substation, same power contract — a higher-value use stacked on the
existing foundation. The analogy is precise.

## Contracted agreements (public, 2026)

| Operator | Agreement | Value |
|---|---|---|
| IREN | Microsoft, 76,000 NVIDIA GPUs | **$9.7B** |
| Hut 8 (HUT) | 15-year lease, 352 MW IT capacity | **$9.8B** base, up to $25.1B with renewals |
| TeraWulf (WULF) | Contracted AI revenue | **$12.8B** |

**Transition evidence:** TeraWulf Q1-2026 reported $21M HPC revenue out of $34M total —
the AI business is already the larger and more stable segment. Sector projections put
AI/HPC at ~70% of transformed-miner revenue by end-2026, against a stated ~$40B revenue
opportunity.

## Why this matters structurally

These are **contracts with investment-grade counterparties**, not narrative exposure.
Compare against every crypto-native "AI infrastructure" token screened in SCREEN-04,
where the dominant pattern was real revenue with 0% capture to the token (Aethir $36M/yr,
Virtuals $21M/yr, both zero). Here, the revenue accrues to equity holders by default —
the capture problem does not exist.

## The trade-off, stated plainly

These are **US-listed equities** (IREN, HUT, WULF, CORZ), not tokens: international
brokerage, FX and tax reporting required. In exchange: signed multi-year contracts with
named counterparties. It is the difference between buying a promise and buying a
contract.

## Where blockchain genuinely fits in this stack

Not as energy — blockchain consumes energy and cannot generate it. Its role is the
coordination layer: agent-to-agent settlement (x402: ~165M transactions, ~$50M cumulative
volume, ~$0.30 average). Real, and embryonic. Bitcoin's own rails cannot serve this
(~7 tps, 10-minute blocks); fast-chain stablecoin settlement can.

## Not yet verified (required before this becomes a thesis)

Debt levels and financing structure of each operator; dilution history; power-cost
contracts; customer concentration; execution risk on delivery timelines. None of these
were checked in this screen.
